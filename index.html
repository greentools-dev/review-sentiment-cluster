Review Mining & Sentiment Clustering FAQ

Why mine customer objections for ad copy?

The highest-converting direct-response advertising directly addresses the primary objection holding back hesitant buyers. Weaponizing customer complaints creates high-trust messaging.

Can I import CSV exports from Shopify or Amazon?

Yes. Click "Upload CSV" and select your review export. The tool automatically detects review body text and star ratings without sending data to external servers.

How is the sentiment distribution calculated?

The engine uses deterministic star ratings (1-2★ negative, 3★ neutral, 4-5★ positive) and keyword emotion scoring to establish percentage distributions.

Is any review data saved on remote servers?

No. The entire text parser and sentiment clustering run inside your browser. If you use BYOK AI, the payload goes directly to Google or OpenAI using your personal API key.
